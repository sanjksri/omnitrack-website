# Surface brief: marketing site (index.html, privacy.html)

Scope: landing page (Persuade) and privacy policy (Read). Audience: non-technical Indian SMB owners deciding whether to trust phone-based tracking and start a trial; drivers arriving for the APK or the policy. Primary action: open the console at app.easyomnitracker.in. Secondary: download the driver APK. Proof is product facts only; every live visual is labelled as a simulation. User chose the category standard (clean modern SaaS), with Samsara and Fleetx as the craft bar, in their pinned palette: deep forest green, warm neutrals, muted amber.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Show the trip, not the tracker. The page proves the mechanism (ID + passcode, driver opens a link, manager watches) with working simulated UI; it refuses the dark neon-map SaaS hero and the icon-card grid.
OWN-WORLD: Warm stone ground, white panels with hairline warm borders, deep forest green for type, primary actions and one full-bleed consent band, muted amber reserved for live state (van, pings, idle). Grotesk display, humanist body, mono only for telemetry. Authored SVG icons, one stroke.
STORY: Understands the three-step setup, believes drivers are tracked only with consent, sees exact pricing, starts free.
FIRST VIEWPORT (revised on user request): hierarchy 1) headline + description, 2) forest primary "Start free" and outlined secondary "Driver app (Android)", 3) a looping golden-hour highway film (rendered, rear view of a white van on a long road) in one large rounded frame on the right, no text on it, 4) a smartphone in the foreground overlapping the film's lower-left corner (lower-right on phones) showing the live map in follow mode on the same kind of highway as the film: Van 04 marker, amber trail, blinking GPS dot, speed and true trip distance, labelled "Simulated trip", 5) a small fact line under the CTAs. Desktop two columns, text left; tablet and mobile stack in the same order. One motion control pauses film and phone.
FORM: canon (standing exit, user-chosen); grounded list position n/a; seed:deff9cb0aecceca7
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction (revised on user request): the phone's map is in follow mode on an open highway corridor that matches the film's road (bends on the same 8 s rhythm). It runs on the film's playhead at the film's 12 m/s: speed 43 km/h, Trip accumulating that true distance, amber trail behind, dotted road ahead, a ping every 1.6 s, blinking GPS dot. The van in the film carries the fleet number 04. Motion grammar: film settles from visible, phone rises in once; afterwards only the film loop and the phone trip move, under one pause control. Fallback and reduced motion: the poster stays, the marker steps between pings. The video and phone never overlap the heading, description, CTAs or fact line at any width.
