What you get in the console

One place to watch, look back and report on every vehicle and field phone you track.

See every active device on one live map

Moving, idle or paused, each device shows its latest position. Zoom to fit the whole fleet, or follow one vehicle as it moves.

Scrub back through any day

Pick a date range and the route history is split into separate trips automatically. A new trip starts whenever the device has been idle for more than 5 minutes.

Trip reports, calculated for you

For each trip you get total distance, moving hours, idle time, and average and top speed. Download it as CSV to work with in Excel or your own tools.

Questions people ask before signing up

Still unsure? Email easyomnitracker@gmail.com.

Why choose easyomnitracker?

It is easy, works anywhere and is reliable. Anyone can set it up in a few clicks without technical knowledge. There's no hardware to fit, no app to install for browser tracking, and it works on any smartphone with minimal registration.

What about the driver's privacy?

Location is tracked only while tracking is switched on, because that is the purpose of the app. No personal information (names, phone numbers or ID documents) is collected or stored, so there is no personal profile to misuse. The driver must tick a consent box before tracking starts, and can stop it at any time.

Why use this instead of AirTag, JioTag or WhatsApp?

Consumer tags are made for personal use, pair with one specific phone, and don't keep proper history. WhatsApp location works well for a small group of people. A business needs many devices tracked at once, managed from one place, with reports and historical records.

How long is my data kept?

Location history is stored securely for up to 6 months. You can download it as CSV at any time for your own records and analysis.

Who uses it?

It's used to monitor commercial fleets, delivery staff, security guards, field sales teams and more.

What if a driver leaves? Can they still send locations?

Technically yes, if they still have the link and passcode, though nobody gains from logging their own location. To cut off access completely, change the passcode for that Tracking ID from your console, and the old passcode stops working.

Is there a referral program?

Yes. With Refer and Earn you get a 30% commission on every payment your referrals make, up to ₹1,500 per referral. It's currently open to Gmail account holders only.

For example, if your referral signs up and spends ₹4,000, you earn ₹1,200. If they pay another ₹4,000 later, you earn ₹300 more, reaching the ₹1,500 cap for that referral.

Payouts are made after the month ends, by the 5th of the next month.

Browser link or Android app: which does the driver need?

If the driver can keep the web page open on screen during the trip, the browser link is enough. If they need to minimise the browser, use other apps or turn the screen off, they should install the Android app: Download Android app (APK) v1.0.3 build 6.

Can someone be tracked without knowing?

No. That is neither legal nor possible with this app. The device owner has to explicitly agree to be tracked and can stop at any time.
